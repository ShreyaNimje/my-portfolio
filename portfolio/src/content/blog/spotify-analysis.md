## 📖 Overview
Spotify Analysis is a data exploration project that digs into personal and aggregate streaming metrics to uncover listening patterns, genre trends, and behavioral insights. Raw streaming data is cleaned, transformed, and visualized to tell a clear story about music consumption habits over time.

### ✨ Features
* **Listening Trend Analysis:** Uncovers patterns across time, genre, and artist.
* **Data Cleaning Pipeline:** Handles raw export data into an analysis-ready format.
* **Rich Visualizations:** Charts and graphs highlighting top artists, tracks, and habits.
* **Genre Breakdown:** Distribution analysis across music categories.
* **Exportable Insights:** Summary visuals ready for sharing or reporting.
